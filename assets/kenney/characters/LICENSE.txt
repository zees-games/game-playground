Source: Kenney Game Assets All-in-1 (kenney.itch.io/kenney-game-assets), bought
2026-09-05. CC0 requires no attribution; this file records provenance anyway so the
next reader knows these are not ours and do not need to be.

All 18 GLBs share byte-identical geometry (leg-left, leg-right, torso, arm-left,
arm-right, head) and differ ONLY by the 1024x1024 atlas in Textures/. That is why
`tools/build_avatar_parts.gd` can generate 18 bodies and 18 heads from them without
touching a mesh, and why the whole set costs about 2.3 MB.
