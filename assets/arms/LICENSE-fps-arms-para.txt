What was changed (art-src/arms/build_arms.py): only the right arm is kept (the left hand is its mirror, drawn in code); the author's IK and copy-rotation control bones and constraints are dropped, clavicle and deltoid weights are merged into the upper arm and the bones are renamed, leaving 22 deform bones that ZeeRiggedArm poses; the skin texture is dropped and the mesh is split into a Glove and a Sleeve surface that the game paints in its own colours; the forearm is thickened slightly and given a flared cuff; the whole arm is scaled to a 0.52 m reach with the shoulder at the origin.
